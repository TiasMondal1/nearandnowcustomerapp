## 7. Dev-mode unlock recipe (for the human doing §8)

Full reference: `docs/DEV_MODE.md`.

1. **Trigger the version toast.** Physical device, release/preview build: shake it deliberately — three sharp peaks within about a second (≥ 1.2 g each, ≥ 250 ms apart, inside 1.2 s; 2 s cooldown). Simulator, `__DEV__` build (RN's dev menu owns the shake there) or web: open the account sheet from the avatar in any tab header and hold the footer brand line for 3 s; the sheet closes, then the toast appears. The toast reads `Near & Now v1.0.1 (5)` for the current config (`expo.version` / `android.versionCode`), is neutral, and plays no haptic or sound.
2. **Tap the toast 5 times within 3 s** of the first tap. Taps pause the toast timer; a swipe does not count; a stale window restarts the count.
3. **Enter the PIN** in the "Developer access" sheet: `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEV_PANEL_PIN` || `extra.devPanelPin` || `7391`. Three wrong attempts → 30 s lockout with a live countdown (persisted; survives a relaunch).
4. On success: success haptic + chime, the sheet dismisses, the "Developer" panel opens one frame later. The near-black DEV pill now floats bottom-right (dot colour = build profile; amber badge = number of changed flags; red "PIN source: default" in the Env tab means the env PIN is not set).
5. Flags live in the **Flags** tab (verbatim `Dev_<Feature>_inhibit_<Target>` names; per-row reset; "Filter flags…"). Any `simulates` flag off its default lights the **2 px amber stripe** under the status bar. **Actions → Lock dev mode** clears the unlock and resets every flag.
6. In `__DEV__` builds the pill is visible from the start but locked (lock glyph); tapping it goes straight to the PIN sheet.
