## What was cropped out, and why

- `aisle-spices.webp`: the source shows a stall-keeper's torso (top right) and a blurred blue shape (top left). The crop starts below and to the left of both.
- `aisle-staples.webp`: several sacks in the source carry printed lettering. The crop keeps only sacks with no print.
- `aisle-vegetables.webp`: the source has chalk price boards at the top and a crate with handwriting on the right. The crop keeps the carrots and the burlap below them.
- `aisle-bakery.webp`: a plain tub and a jar lid at the top left of the source are outside the crop.
- `empty-cart.webp`: an out-of-focus jar at the top left of the source is outside the crop.

## Photos looked at and rejected

Not bundled because they show part of a person (hands or a torso), even without a face: the chai mug, the roti stove, the fruit seller with the scale, the market basket being carried, the pea pod. Not bundled because of handwriting in the frame: the white fruit flat-lay.

## What CC0 does and does not cover

- CC0 waives the photographer's copyright. It gives no model release, property release or trademark permission.
- That is why none of these photos shows a person, a face, a shop sign, a brand name or branded packaging. Keep it that way for every photo added later.
- Many of these Commons pages also sit in a maintenance category named "Images from Unsplash (review needed)". The licence tag on each page is CC0; what that review covers was not established.

## Rules for adding a photo

1. Licence: CC0 or public domain only. Nothing "free for personal use", nothing that requires a link back inside the app.
2. No people (not even hands), no readable third-party text or signage, no brand packaging.
3. Record file, where it is used, author, source page, licence and the crop in the table above.
4. Sizes: portrait 360 x 432, tile 480 x 600, wide 1080 x 480, WebP quality 78. Keep all of `assets/photos/` under 1.5 MB.
